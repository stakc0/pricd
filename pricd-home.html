THE MODEL

Price is a conversation
between market data and demand.

01

Index

We cache exact-mint market data, images, volume, liquidity, and price history in the database.

02

Estimate

Market cap sets the baseline. Volume and momentum adjust the estimated sale range.

03

Verify

Wallet signatures prove the seller controls the coin before a listing can go live.

04

Bid

Buyers submit offers. A market maker can keep a visible bid and ask around the estimate.
